A terminal for the trenches.

Screen Solana memecoins from public, keyless data, and let your own claude or codex login read the same screens. Paper trading by default.


          
macOS and Linux. No sudo; fetches a verified Node if yours is missing.

        

      

    

  


  

    

      

        

          

            
          

        

      

      

        
    
        
    
          
    Sample data. Every frame is drawn by trenchscope itself, cell for cell.
    
          
        
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    Your analyst reads the screen you are reading.
    
      
    Press a and your own claude or codex login gets the same tool results, through a read-only MCP server.
    
      
    
        
    Labelled
    Every answer is marked model output, with the age of every number it used.
    
        
    Untrusted input
    Token names, descriptions and fetched pages reach the model wrapped as untrusted text.
    
        
    Read-only
    It has no build, sign or send tool. The most it can do is open a paper ticket.
    
        
    Your login
    Usage goes to your own account. Everything else works without it.
    
      
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    Keyboard only.
    
      
    Every screen answers to the same few keys. Press ? for the rest.
    
    
    
    
      
      
    • jk
      Move through rows on any list.
      • 
      
    • enter
      Open the coin: chart, holders, tape and safety.
      • 
      
    • a
      Ask the analyst about what is on screen.
      • 
      
    • /
      Filter with plain thresholds: vol24 > 250K liq > 50K
      • 
      
    • w
      Watch a coin and set alerts, stored on your machine.
      • 
      
    • c
      Chart in a docked window or your browser.
      • 
      
    • esc
      Back to where you came from.
      • 
      
    • q
      Quit. Nothing keeps running.
      • 
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    It never holds your keys.
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Paper first
    Every run starts on the simulated wallet. Live is devnet unless you start with --mainnet.
    
        
    Signing
    Nothing is sent without a keypress and your wallet's approval.
    
        
    Caps
    A separate signer refuses anything over your per-trade and daily caps.
    
        
    The model
    The analyst has no build, sign or send tool.
    
        
    Network
    Local servers bind 127.0.0.1 only.
    
        
    Telemetry
    None. No account, no crash reports, no update check.
    
      
    
      
    Known issues. Paper and live trading are experimental and have bugs we are still fixing. Do not rely on them yet. Nothing here is financial advice.
