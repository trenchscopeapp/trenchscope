# Code of conduct

dexterm's issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any space the maintainers run are for working on the software. Everyone taking part is expected to be decent to each other.

**Do:** be direct and specific; assume good faith; argue about code and data, not people; welcome newcomers and people who use different tools; accept that maintainers may say no.

**Do not:** harass, insult, threaten or demean anyone; share someone's private information; post sexualised or violent content; spam, shill tokens, or use the project's spaces to pump anything; impersonate maintainers or other members.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content and may warn, temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, at their discretion. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by messaging a maintainer on GitHub; reports are handled confidentially.

This document is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, shortened for a small project.
