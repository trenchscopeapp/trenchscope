`K` opens your lists. `X` there (or `dexterm kol import-x`) imports the lists your X account **owns** as `X LIST` entries: handles only, never posts, and they count as tier A for credibility like your own manual lists. `o` on a handle narrows the trending board and the SOCIAL panel to that handle's posts (press `o` again, or `f`, to clear). Muting a handle (`m`) drops its posts before they are stored.
