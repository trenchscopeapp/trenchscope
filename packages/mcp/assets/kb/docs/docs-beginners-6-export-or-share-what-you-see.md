Press `E` to export the current screen. An **export** is a saved copy: dexterm writes a PNG image (a common image-file format) and a plain-text report under `~/.dexterm/exports/`. The `~` means your home folder. Nothing is uploaded or posted.

Open that folder with your normal file manager if you want to share the files yourself. Check the image and text before sending them; a live coin view may contain a public wallet address or other details you would rather hide. dexterm never posts for you.
