Default answers are **3 to 6 short lines**, written for a narrow panel beside the screener, no recap of what is already on screen, no boilerplate:

1. the call: `LONG|SHORT|WAIT|AVOID <SYMBOL> · conf <0-100> · setup strong|mixed|weak`
2. why: the one or two tool facts that decide it and that you cannot already see
3. levels: entry zone · invalidation (and why it sits there) · target
4. size: risk as a % of account, stop at the invalidation; dollar math only when you state an account size
5. at most one risk line that changes the trade

Long form (sections, a table, at most about 25 lines) only when you ask with words like "full", "detail", "deep dive" or "break it down". The analyst is instructed never to narrate tool calls, never to pad with disclaimers (the app shows its own footer), and never to refuse a normal trading question: WAIT and AVOID are calls. Confidence is 0–100 in prose and 0–1 in the structured skills.

> warn: model output is a model's reading of tool results. It can be wrong, incomplete or stale, and it is not financial advice. The app labels it as model output with data ages; treat it as a starting point for your own checking.
